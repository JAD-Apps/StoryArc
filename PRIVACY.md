# StoryArc privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026.

StoryArc is a Windows audiobook player developed by John Donnelly, trading as
JAD Apps. Your audio, library and listening history are processed on your PC.
StoryArc does not upload them to JAD Apps or a cloud transcription service.

## Data stored on your PC

StoryArc stores the information needed to run the player in your Windows user
profile, in its AudioBookPlayer application-data folder:

- Library file paths, titles, authors, durations, chapter information and cover art.
- Playback position, bookmarks, speed, volume, caption choices and listening statistics.
- Library folders, display preferences and audio-effect settings.
- Downloaded Whisper speech-recognition models, when you choose to use them.
- Diagnostic logs describing operations and errors, which can include local file paths.
  Logs are retained locally for 14 days and are not sent automatically.

External subtitle files and your original audiobooks remain where you keep them.
Removing a book from StoryArc's library does not delete the original audio file.
Application data may remain after uninstalling, depending on the installation and
Windows data-retention settings.

## Network activity

Generated captions require a speech-recognition model. When you request a model
that is not installed, StoryArc downloads it over HTTPS using Whisper.net's model
host. The host receives normal connection information, such as your IP address.
The request does not include your audio, document contents, library or listening
history. Caption generation then runs locally.

Opening the JAD Apps website, this policy or a support link launches your browser.
Those sites have their own privacy policies. StoryArc does not add audiobook
contents or tracking parameters to these links.

StoryArc has no account system, advertising, analytics or automatic crash-reporting
service. It does not upload diagnostic logs. You can choose to share information
when asking for support.

## Permissions

StoryArc reads the audio files, subtitle files and library folders you select,
plays audio through your chosen output device, and registers supported file types
so Windows can offer StoryArc in its Open with menu. It does not use your microphone,
camera, location or contacts.

## Children and content

StoryArc does not supply audiobook content. The material available in the player
depends on the files you choose to open.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
For privacy questions, [open a support issue](https://github.com/JAD-Apps/StoryArc/issues)
or use the publisher contact details in the Microsoft Store listing when available.
